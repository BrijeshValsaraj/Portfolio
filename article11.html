Nobody came to my Funeral

Brijesh Valsaraj

← My Writings

Nobody came to my funeral because
they buried me long before I died
In every call they ignored,
I disappeared a little bit more

My smile became the language nobody bothered to learn.
The flowers arrived too late;
I had already wilted in silence.
The loudest grief was the love I never received.

Even the wind knew my name better
than the people I called home.
So the earth held me gently,
at last, something finally did.
